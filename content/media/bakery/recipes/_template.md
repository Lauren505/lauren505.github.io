# Matcha roll

Portion: one 25 cm roll, about 8 slices

## Ingredients

### Sponge

- 4 eggs, separated
- 70 g caster sugar
- 50 g cake flour
- 8 g matcha

### Cream

- 200 ml whipping cream
- 15 g sugar

## Steps

1. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar to a soft meringue.
2. Fold in the yolks, then the flour and matcha, sifted together.
3. Spread on a lined tray and bake at 180 °C for 10–12 minutes.
4. Whip the cream with the sugar, spread it over the cooled sponge, and roll.
