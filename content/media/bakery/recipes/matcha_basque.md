# Basque Burnt Cheesecake

Size: 6-inch round mold

## Ingredients

### Basque Cheesecake

- cream cheese 400 g
- granulated sugar 112 g
- egg 140 g
- heavy cream 250 g
- cake flour 18.6 g
- vanilla extract 4.5 g
- lemon juice 6 g

## Steps

Regular

1. Bake at 240 °C for 30–35 minutes.
2. The surface should be slightly over-browned while the center remains slightly fluid.

For a softer center

1. Bake at 215 °C for 35 minutes.
2. Reduce the temperature to 150 °C and bake for another 35 minutes, aiming for a center temperature of 65 °C.
3. Cool at room temperature until the cake reaches approximately 48 °C.
4. Refrigerate for 8 hours.
