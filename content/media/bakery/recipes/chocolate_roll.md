# Chocolate Roll

Size: 28 × 28 cm baking tray

## Ingredients

### Cake Roll Sponge

- egg yolks 84 g
- granulated sugar 15 g
- egg whites 133 g
- granulated sugar 54 g
- cake flour 49 g
- cocoa powder 10 g
- unsalted butter 12 g
- milk 15 g

### Chocolate Cream Filling

- heavy cream 113 g
- dark chocolate (75%) 23 g
- white chocolate 8 g

### Chocolate Ganache

- dark chocolate 17 g
- heavy cream 20 g

## Steps

1. Bake at 180 °C for 18 minutes.
2. Spread the cream more thickly along the edge where you will begin rolling.
3. If adding a central filling, place it near the starting edge of the roll.
