# Chocolate Mochi Tart

Size: 7 cm diameter

## Ingredients

### Tart Crust

- unsalted butter 135 g
- granulated sugar 57 g
- egg 45 g
- cake flour 225 g
- almond flour 57 g

### Mochi

- shiratama dango powder 50 g
- water 4.5 g
