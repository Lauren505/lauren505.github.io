# Opéra Cake

Size: 5 rectangular cakes, 11 cm each

## Ingredients

### Coffee Almond Sponge Cake

- egg whites 50 g
- granulated sugar 30 g
- egg 35 g
- powdered sugar 25 g
- almond flour 25 g
- cake flour 22 g
- instant coffee powder 2 g

### Ganache

- chocolate 50 g
- heavy cream 28 g

### Caramel Sauce

- granulated sugar 40 g
- water 15 g
- heavy cream 45 g

### Caramel Buttercream

- caramel sauce 50 g
- unsalted butter 50 g

### Caramel Glaze

- chocolate 20 g
- heavy cream 10 g
- caramel sauce 20 g
