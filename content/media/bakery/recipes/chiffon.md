# Chiffon Cake

Size: 6-inch round mold

## Ingredients

### Chiffon Cake

- cake flour 60 g (or 50 g if add cocoa powder 15 g)
- vegetable oil 30 g
- milk 40 g
- egg yolks 3
- egg whites 3
- lemon juice 1 tsp
- sugar 45 g

## Steps
1. Bake at 150 °C  / 300 °F for 45 minutes (or 150 °C 40 min for a 12cm mold)
