# Mousse

## Ingredients

### Cream Cheese Mousse

- cream cheese 200 g
- granulated sugar 60 g
- milk 85 g
- gelatin 7.5 g
- heavy cream 150 g
- orange zest

### Coffee Mousse

- milk 138 g

- instant coffee powder 8 g
- egg yolks 50 g
- granulated sugar 28 g
- gelatin 5.5 g
- white chocolate 97 g

### Chocolate Mousse

- milk 152 g
- granulated sugar 46 g
- egg yolks 55 g

### Chocolate Hazelnut Mousse

## Steps

Cream Cheese Mousse

1. Soften the cream cheese and beat until smooth and creamy.
2. Add the granulated sugar, followed by the milk, mixing well after each addition.
3. Add the dissolved gelatin and orange zest.
4. Whip the heavy cream to medium-stiff peaks, approximately 70–80% whipped.
5. Fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture until evenly combined.

Chocolate Mousse

1. Mix half of the granulated sugar with the egg yolks. If desired, first temper the yolks with a small amount of the milk and cream.
2. Combine the remaining sugar with the milk and bring to a boil.
3. Gradually pour the hot milk into the egg yolk mixture while stirring.
4. Return the mixture to the saucepan and cook over low heat, stirring continuously with a spatula, until slightly thickened and approximately 83 °C.
5. Remove from the heat and dissolve the softened gelatin in the hot custard.
6. Strain the custard over the chocolate and stir until completely melted and smooth.
7. Cool over an ice bath while stirring to approximately 20 °C. Do not overcool, or the mixture will become too thick.
8. Whip the heavy cream to medium-stiff peaks, approximately 70–80% whipped.
9. Fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture until evenly combined.
