# Macaron

## Ingredients

### Macaron Shells

- almond flour (superfine) 60 g
- powdered sugar 60 g
- egg whites 43 g
- granulated sugar 43 g
- (food coloring, if needed)
- (cocoa powder 6 g, if added, take the same weight off flour)

### Chocolate Ganache Filling

- dark chocolate 80 g
- syrup 10 g
- heavy cream 80 g
- Cointreau 5 g

### Raspberry Chocolate Ganache

- raspberries 100 g, yielding approximately 60 g purée
- granulated sugar 20 g
- white chocolate 50 g
- dark chocolate 35 g
- syrup 10 g
- heavy cream 50 g

## Steps

1. Sift the powdered sugar separately. Do not force coarse almond flour through the sieve; discard the remaining coarse pieces.
2. Macaronage.
3. Pipe the shells to approximately 4 cm in diameter.
4. Dry for 30 minutes (or dry using convection at 40 °C for 15 minutes in a humid environment), until the surface is no longer sticky and springs back gently when pressed.
5. Bake at 150 °C for 7 minutes with the tray on the lowest oven rack. The feet should begin appearing at around the 5-minute mark.
6. Reduce to 140 °C and bake for another 8 minutes.
7. Remove the tray and let the shells cool completely on the baking tray.
