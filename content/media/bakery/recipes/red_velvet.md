# Red Velvet Cake

## Ingredients

### Red Velvet Cake

- all-purpose flour 187.5 g
- cocoa powder 10 g
- salt 2 g
- baking soda 3.75 g
- unsalted butter 71 g
- granulated sugar 125 g
- egg 1 large
- vanilla extract 7.5 g
- buttermilk 150 g
- vinegar 9.4 g
- red food coloring 4 g

### Cream Cheese Frosting

- unsalted butter 95 g
- cream cheese 190 g
- powdered sugar 70 g
- salt 2 g
- heavy cream 60 g

## Steps

1. Bake at 160 °C / 320 °F for 17 minutes.
