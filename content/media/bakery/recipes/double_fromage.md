# Double Fromage

Size: 15 cm / 6-inch round mold

## Ingredients

### Sponge Cake

- eggs 2
- granulated sugar 40 g
- cake flour 50 g
- milk 15 g

### Baked Cheesecake

- cream cheese 225 g
- cake flour 6 g
- granulated sugar 55 g
- egg 1
- heavy cream 47 g

### Mascarpone Cheesecake

- egg yolk 1
- granulated sugar 47 g
- water 25 g
- mascarpone 100 g
- gelatin powder 5 g
- rum 5 g
- heavy cream 220 g
- vanilla extract 1 drop

## Steps

1. Unmold the baked sponge cake and save the crumbs.
2. Bake the first cheesecake layer at 150 °C for 25 minutes.
3. Let it cool completely, then add the second cheesecake layer and refrigerate until set.
4. Gently spread the crumbs over the surface.
