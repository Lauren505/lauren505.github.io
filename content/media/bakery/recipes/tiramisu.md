# Tiramisu

## Ingredients

### Tiramisu Cream

- egg yolks 40 g
- granulated sugar 40 g
- rum (or water or milk) 15 g
- mascarpone 340 g (optionally replace some with heavy cream)
- vanilla extract 1 tsp

### Tiramisu Cream (Mr. Cheesecake's Recipe)

- mascarpone 137 g
- cream cheese 34 g
- sweetened condensed milk 14 g
- unsalted butter 7 g
- heavy cream 137 g
- hojicha tea 1.4 g
- white chocolate 14 g
- egg yolks 27 g
- granulated sugar 21 g
- egg whites 41 g
- granulated sugar 14 g

### Coffee Syrup

- espresso 100 g
- granulated sugar 12 g
- Kahlúa 12 g
