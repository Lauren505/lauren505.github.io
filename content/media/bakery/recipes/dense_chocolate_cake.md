# Gâteau au Chocolat de Yann Couvreur

Size: 6-inch round mold

## Ingredients

### Chocolate Cake

- egg 180 g, about 3.6 large eggs
- granulated sugar 90 g
- granulated sugar 6 g
- dark chocolate 120 g
- milk chocolate 120 g
- unsalted butter 120 g
- all-purpose flour 60 g
- fleur de sel 3.6 g
- unsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting

## Steps

1. Melt the dark chocolate, milk chocolate, and butter together over a bain-marie until smooth.
2. Add the fleur de sel and let the mixture cool slightly.
3. Whisk the eggs, granulated sugar, and vanilla sugar until slightly foamy.
4. Gradually fold the chocolate mixture into the egg mixture.
5. Sift in the flour and fold just until combined.
6. Pour into the prepared springform pan.
7. Bake at 145 °C for approximately 35 minutes.
8. Cool completely before unmolding.
9. Dust with unsweetened cocoa powder, if desired.

## Notes

This could work well as a thin chocolate base layer for an entremet.
